00–06 seconds / Meet TapCPeso
Meet Tap C Peso, an offline wallet prototype using demo money.

06–14 seconds / 01 / Set up
Tap Quick Demo Setup. Open Person One on the first phone, and Person Two on the second.

14–22 seconds / 02 / Add money
Your dashboard shows the local balance. Add mock funds, then choose Send Offline.

22–31 seconds / 03 / Authorize
Enter the receiver's number and twenty five pesos. Confirm your wallet PIN, then approve with your fingerprint or device passcode.

31–40 seconds / 04 / Transfer
Funds are deducted once. The receiver scans your QR, or reads NFC from a compatible Android sender.

40–48 seconds / 05 / Receive
The app checks the recipient and signature, then credits the receiver. Duplicate payments are rejected locally.

48–55 seconds / 06 / Review and sync
History starts as offline pending. Simulated sync verifies signatures. It does not contact a bank.

55–60 seconds / TapCPeso
Tap C Peso: demo money only. Hardware testing required.
